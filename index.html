Strong, connected, Christ-centered families.

Our mission is to glorify God by connecting families, strengthening marriages, and equipping homes with biblical principles to follow Christ. We serve both English and Russian-speaking families.

Connect
We create opportunities for families and couples to build genuine relationships, connect with the broader church body, and develop a strong sense of belonging through fellowship.
Strengthen
We provide couples and families with personal support, structured biblical guidance, and mentorship to strengthen marriages and navigate life together.
Equip
We help families grow spiritually through biblical teaching, Christ-centered guidance, and intentional opportunities to apply God’s Word to your home.
